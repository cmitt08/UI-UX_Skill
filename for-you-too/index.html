How your number works

  • Your number is a budget, not a punishment. It's what your body burns in a day minus a gentle gap. Eat your number most days and the 10–15 lbs happens quietly by New Year's Eve — no starving, no weird rules.
  • Whole foods are the whole trick. Real food fills you up on fewer calories, so the gap never really feels like one. The library below only stocks the real stuff — if it needs an ingredient list, it didn't make the cut.
  • Three meals, no snacks — because that's how you like it. That structure is genuinely great for locking in, so the whole site is built around it. There's a switch for flexible days, and zero pressure either way.
  • Protein is the fullness cheat code. You said it yourself — it fills you up, and it protects the muscle you're building. Your protein goal sits right next to your calorie number, the green ring tracks it, and "some at every meal" is the whole strategy.
  • I will never send you below 1,200 a day. That's the floor and it's a promise. And if a day ever feels off — dizzy, dragging, hangry at strangers — eat more that day. The plan bends. You don't have to.

    Three things I need you to know

    The scale is still a liar

    Water, salt, sleep, and the moon push it around by a couple of pounds for no reason. Weigh weekly, trust the trend line only — and remember: I think you're perfect at every number on it.

    One meal never ruined anything

    An over-budget dinner is information, not a crime. You don't earn food and you never owe penance. The next meal is the reset, and it's always right there.

    Eat enough, lose slow

    You're still lifting — that muscle is what makes this whole thing work, and it needs feeding. Slow isn't a consolation prize. Slow is the version that lasts.
