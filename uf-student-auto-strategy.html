We can't get auto customers from car dealerships because new-car buyers already have insurance. First cars. First leases. Florida vehicle registration. First policies in their own name. Nobody in Gainesville systematically owns those moments. We can.

The Core Insight

A new-car buyer at a dealership already has coverage. A 19-year-old registering a car in Florida to qualify for in-state tuition, signing her first Gainesville lease, or graduating into her first job does not. Students compress more forced-insurance moments into four years than most adults hit in twenty.

Cheap to win

No ad budget needed — these channels run on legwork, partnerships, and one-page handouts.

Compounding value

Renters today, auto at graduation, home in five years. We capture customers at the start of the ladder.

Parents come attached

Parents co-sign and pay. One student policy regularly opens a whole multi-car household.

How We Reach Them

Six plays, in priority order

1

The Residency Play

Out-of-state students chasing in-state tuition must build Florida ties: FL license, FL vehicle registration — and Florida registration requires Florida insurance. Parents' out-of-state policy often can't follow the car. No one markets to this.

Run it
  • Build a one-page "Becoming a Florida Resident" checklist — insurance is simply step 3, with our QR code.
  • Seed it where students search: off-campus housing offices, the UF subreddit, class GroupMe chats.
2

The Lease-Signing Play

Nearly every off-campus complex requires renters insurance at lease signing. A ~$15/month renters policy is our foot in the door — and the auto bundle pitch is built in.

Run it
  • Visit leasing offices along Archer Rd, SW 13th & Sorority Row with "covered in 10 minutes" cards for lease packets.
  • Give leasing staff same-day proof of coverage — we solve their compliance chase.
  • Table at UF's off-campus housing fairs each spring and summer.
3

The Scooter Play

Gainesville runs on scooters. Local dealers sell to students year-round; financed scooters need coverage and parents usually pay. Every scooter policy opens a household full of cars.

Run it
  • Partner with the main student scooter dealers (e.g., New Scooters 4 Less, Solano Cycle) — same-day proof promise.
  • Cross-sell every scooter household: "Want me to run your auto while I'm in here? Bundling helps both."
4

The Parent Play

Parents pay the premiums — and many out-of-state carriers can't properly cover a car garaged and registered in Florida. Gator parent Facebook groups field insurance questions every single week.

Run it
  • Be visible when parents are in town: move-in weekend and Family Weekend (water station, branded help table).
  • Become the helpful local expert in Gator parent groups — answer questions, never spam.
  • One-pager: "Your student's car is 300 miles from home. Is it actually covered?"
5

The Graduation Play

Every May and December, thousands of grads take first jobs and leave their parents' policies — a guaranteed first-policy wave, twice a year, forever.

Run it
  • "Your First Policy 101" one-pager with QR code at career-prep events and senior weeks.
  • Collect renewal dates from spring seniors; auto renews every 6 months, so every "no" comes back twice a year.
6

The Ambassador Play

Businesses can't table on Plaza of the Americas — but registered student orgs can. Students trust students, and a sponsorship costs less than one month of ads.

Run it
  • Sponsor a business fraternity, sales club, or Gator Motorsports in exchange for tabling partnership & referrals.
  • Keep any per-referral thank-yous inside Florida's gift rules (see compliance note, p.3).

When To Strike

The Gainesville calendar does the marketing for us

June – July
UF Preview (orientation) — parents are on campus with checkbooks open. Prep materials now; contact leasing offices & scooter dealers before the rush.
August
Move-in week — the single biggest wave of the year: new leases, new scooters, cars newly garaged in Florida. Renters + auto bundles; parent one-pagers everywhere.
Sept – Nov
Game days & Family Weekend — parents return; campus is at full energy. Parent play + ambassador tabling.
December
Fall graduation — first-policy wave #1. Spring lease signings also begin for next year.
January
Spring arrivals & lease renewals — transfer students and a second, smaller move-in. Re-run the August playbook at lower volume.
April – May
Spring graduation — the biggest first-policy wave of all. Capture renewal dates from every senior we talk to.
