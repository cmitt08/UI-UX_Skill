How this plan gets you toned

  • "Toned" = a little muscle + a little less fluff. The weights do the shaping. The cardio and eating well do the leaning. You need both, and this plan has both.
  • You will not get bulky. Promise. Building big muscle takes years of heavy lifting and eating for it. This plan uses lighter weights and higher reps — it firms and shapes, it doesn't bulk.
  • How heavy? Pick a weight where the last 3 reps of each set feel like real work but your form stays pretty (like you). If you could chat through all 15 reps, go a little heavier next set.
  • Rest 45–60 seconds between sets. Long enough to breathe, short enough to keep your heart rate up. Sip water. Text me.

Saturday + Sunday are rest days — stretch, go for a walk, live your beautiful life.

The honest stuff

Three things I need you to know

Consistency beats perfection

A "meh" workout you actually did beats a perfect one you skipped. Show up 4 out of 5 days and you're winning. Miss a day? Just pick up where you left off. No guilt allowed on this website.

Protein is your best friend

Toned needs protein to work with. Aim for some at every meal — eggs, chicken, greek yogurt, a shake, whatever you like. It also keeps you full, which makes the "lose a little" part way easier.

The scale is a liar

Muscle is compact, so you can get visibly more toned while the scale barely moves. Judge by how clothes fit, how you feel, and photos — not that number. (For the record: I think you're perfect at every number.)
